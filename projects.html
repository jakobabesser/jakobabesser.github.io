Selected Projects

  • NeuroSensEar (Carl Zeiss Foundation, 2023–2028, PI)
    Neuromorphic acoustic sensing for the next generation of hearing aids.
  • BioMonitor4CAP (EU, 2022–2026, PM)
    Advanced biodiversity monitoring, including acoustic methods, for effective agricultural policy.
  • ISAD / ISAD 2 (DFG, 2018–2024, PI)
    Informed sound activity detection in music and audio recordings.
  • SoundSensor (FhG, 2024–2025, PI)
    Acoustic sensors for noise and traffic measurement.
  • Stadtlärm (BMWi, 2016–2018, PM)
    A distributed system for urban noise monitoring and noise-source identification.
  • Jazzomat (DFG, 2012–2017)
    Music-information-retrieval methods for the analysis of jazz improvisation.
  • SyncGlobal (BMBF, 2011–2013)
    Global music search applied to cross-modal synchronization with video content.
  • GlobalMusic2one (BMBF, 2008–2010)
    Adaptive hybrid search technologies for global music collections.
